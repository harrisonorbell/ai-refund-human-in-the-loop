# Coastal Electronics — Refund Approval Rules

## Automatic Approval

A refund can be automatically approved only when:

- Refund amount is $50 or less.
- Purchase was made within the last 30 days.
- Refund reason is eligible under company policy.
- All required information is present.
- No investigation or policy exception is required.

## Manager Review

A refund must be sent for human review when:

- Refund amount exceeds $50.
- Purchase is older than 30 days.
- The customer reports missing or damaged delivery.
- Information is missing or unclear.
- The AI assessment indicates uncertainty or elevated risk.

## AI Responsibilities

- Categorise the customer's refund reason.
- Summarise the request.
- Identify potential risks or unusual circumstances.
- Recommend an action.

## Human Responsibilities

- Review escalated requests.
- Approve or reject requests.
- Provide a reason for the decision.
- Override AI recommendations when appropriate.

## Automation Responsibilities

- Enforce the business rules.
- Track request statuses.
- Prevent duplicate decisions.
- Record manager decisions.
- Notify customers of outcomes.
- Maintain an audit trail.
